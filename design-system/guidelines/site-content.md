## About Us
Lesotho Stone Enterprises is a mature mining Company operating in the region of Lakaokaneng, Lesotho for over 20 years, and is the largest quarry of Natural Sandstone building products in Southern Africa cut from the finest sandstone resources in the Kingdom of Lesotho.
Specialist, precision cutting machines and a skilled team of stone masons offer some of the most advanced stone mining technology and expertise, assuring productivity in excess of 200,000 square meters of Natural Sandstone cut, produced and supplied annually.
In addition to volume production of our standard product range, we also have the capability to produce an extensive range of custom designed products to suit specific building and architectural and specifications.
We have country wide market coverage and distribution channels which include, the greater Gauteng Region, Free state, KwaZulu Natal, Eastern Cape, Garden Route and Western Cape, and soon into neighboring African Countries.

## Distribution
Our sandstone mine is based in the Lekokoaneng region of Lesotho and serves as the production and distribution centre for our extensive range of Sandstone Products which are exported into South Africa and other African countries.
